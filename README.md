## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/People/Woman%20Technologist.png" width="28"/> About

I'm a Computer Science graduate finishing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence Engineering at **ISEP (Polytechnic Institute of Porto)**, with a Bachelor's from the **Federal Institute of Maranhão**, Brazil.

My focus sits at the intersection of **applied AI research and full-stack engineering** — designing retrieval architectures, autonomous agent systems, and computer vision pipelines, then shipping them as real, usable products. As the founder of a technology startup, I've taken ideas from architecture diagram to deployed web and mobile platform end-to-end.

I'm currently researching **GraphRAG** (graph-based retrieval-augmented generation) for my Master's thesis, focused on improving LLM accuracy and explainability over fragmented enterprise data.

**The Solution**
A Graph-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GraphRAG) architecture designed to replace traditional vector search.

**Focus areas**
- Improving LLM accuracy and contextual relevance
- Explainability of results for enterprise query resolution
- Reasoning over fragmented, disconnected data sources

**What it demonstrates**
Applied AI research translated into a practical enterprise search architecture.
